关于 / AboutAbout

赖欣 / Xin LaiXin Lai

我目前是北京雁栖湖应用数学研究院（BIMSA）概率、应用统计和数据科学团队的博士后研究员。我于中国人民大学获得应用数学博士学位，研究兴趣包括图上的离散 Ricci 曲率、Ricci 流、图神经网络与网络结构。I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Probability, Applied Statistics and Data Science Group at the Beijing Institute of Mathematical Sciences and Applications (BIMSA). I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from Renmin University of China. My research interests include discrete Ricci curvature, Ricci flow on graphs, graph neural networks, and network structure.

我也关注数学与 AI 方法在计算生物学中的交叉应用。I am also interested in interdisciplinary applications of mathematics and AI in computational biology.
